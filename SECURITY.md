# Security

anynear carries encrypted data of real people. If you find a weakness in anynear – the apps, the web app, the
protocols ANMP and ANDP, the relays or the website – please tell us before you make it public.

This policy applies to every repository of the anynear organisation that has no `SECURITY.md` of its own.

## Reporting

Write to **[security@anynear.app](mailto:security@anynear.app)**, in English or German. Describe what you observed and
how it can be reproduced. Please do not include other people's data, invitation links or backup files, and do not open
a public issue or pull request for a vulnerability.

- We confirm receipt and tell you what happens next.
- Please give us time to fix the problem before you publish it.
- Test only with your own devices, circles and keys, and do not disrupt the relays of the anynear network.
- We cannot pay a bounty. If you wish, we name you once the problem is fixed.

The contact is also available in machine-readable form at https://anynear.app/.well-known/security.txt.

## Known limits

anynear has not been independently audited yet. The procedures it uses and their known limits – for example no forward
secrecy and what the relays can see – are described at https://anynear.app/en/technology/. These are not
vulnerabilities to report, but comments on them are welcome.

For anything that is not about security, write to [support@anynear.app](mailto:support@anynear.app).
